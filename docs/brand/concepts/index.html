Pashu Shield — mark concepts

Three original, single-colour, geometric directions (DBIM 5; brief §2.1). No national symbols, no gradients, no photography, no 3D. Pick one — the winner gets finalised into SVG masters, outlined wordmark, lockups, favicons, PWA icons and social card, plus the Logo component, /brand page and rule tests.

Concept art uses layered knockouts for presentation; the finalised mark ships as true single-colour artwork (compound path / mask).

Concept A — Horn Shield

Idea: a solid protection shield with cattle horns and a muzzle dot in negative space. Reads as “livestock under protection” even at 30 px. Most literal for farmers.

Risk: horns + dot is a common livestock trope — ownability rests on the exact geometry.

Concept B — Pasture Shield

Idea: shield + leaf over a ground line — animal husbandry (grazing, growth, care of the herd). Calmest and most distinctive silhouette of the three.

Risk: reads “agri/organic” first, “animal health” second — the wordmark must carry the veterinary meaning.

Concept C — Ear Tag

Idea: a livestock ear-tag (the universal on-farm ID object) with a knocked-out hole and ID slot. Zero shield cliché, instantly “livestock”, and it owns the QR/animal-ID story the platform already has.

Risk: drops the “shield = protection” metaphor — protection is carried by the wordmark instead.
